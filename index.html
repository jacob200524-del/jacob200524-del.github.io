💡 The 5 rules that keep it cheap

Air frying saves money only if you shop for it on purpose. These are the rules every recipe here follows.

  • 1
    Buy overlap, not recipesOne protein + one starch + one veg should cover 2–3 meals. If an ingredient appears once, skip the recipe.
  • 2
    Frozen is your friendFrozen wings, fish, and broccoli air-fry beautifully and cost 30–50% less than fresh. Thaw, pat very dry, season.
  • 3
    Never crowd the basketOne layer, space between pieces. Crowding steams food — that's how you end up ordering takeout anyway.
  • 4
    Shake or flip at halfwaySet a timer. The flip is the difference between “pretty good” and the photo on Pinterest.
  • 5
    Season after, sauce lastSugary sauces burn at 400°F. Cook seasoned, toss in sauce in the last 2 minutes or right after.

Quick questions

Do I need to preheat the air fryer?

For breaded foods and anything you want truly crisp — yes, 3–5 minutes at cooking temperature. For vegetables and reheating leftovers, you can skip it and add 2 minutes.

Can I cook frozen chicken directly?

Wings and tenders, yes — add about 50% more time and season after the surface thaws (around the halfway flip). Whole breasts are better thawed so the outside doesn't burn before the center cooks. Always verify 165°F inside.

Why isn't my food crispy?

Almost always one of three things: the basket was crowded, the food was wet (pat it dry!), or there was no oil at all. A light spray matters — air alone can't brown a dry coating.

Are these costs real?

They're careful estimates from typical U.S. supermarket shelf prices (store brands, family packs), calculated per serving in November 2026. Salmon and fish swing the most by region and season — when they're pricey, swap in the chicken or chickpea recipes that week.
